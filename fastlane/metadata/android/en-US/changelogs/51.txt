## What's Changed
* Added support for Memos 0.31.0.
* Added support for restoring local backups.
* Added support for transferring local memos and attachments to a server account by @Ekats.
* Added client certificate support for mTLS by @tkutsuya.
* Added Ukrainian localization by @draskold.
* Added support for smaller home screen widgets by @bbohue.
* Improved autosave reliability and fixed edits being lost when syncing or leaving the editor by @Ekats.
* Fixed pinned and archived status being lost when memos are first uploaded by @Ekats.
* Fixed widgets not refreshing after editing memos by @Ekats.
* Fixed duplicate attachment uploads when saving memos.
