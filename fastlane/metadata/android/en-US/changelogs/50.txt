## What's Changed
* Added support for Memos 0.30.0.
* Added optional autosave for the memo editor by @Ekats.
* Added a side-by-side memo list and detail view on larger screens.
* Updated dependencies and added support for Android 17 local network permissions.
* Improved search, settings, photo picking, and accessibility.
* Improved opening memos from widgets and shortcuts by @Ekats.
* Fixed local edits being lost when syncing by @Ekats.
