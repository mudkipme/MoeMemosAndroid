## What's Changed
* Added support for Memos 26.10 and 26.11 by @cercle10.
* Added support for attaching a location to memos.
* Added in-app language selection by @fastmind2014.
* Added an option to include archived memos in search results by @the-great-bear.
* Improved tag detection so tags stop at punctuation and escaped tags are ignored.
* Improved memo preview length for CJK text by @PC267.
* Fixed app lock crashes on Android 9 and 10.
* Added Vietnamese localization by @dinhkarate.
* Refined German translations by @bttrx.
